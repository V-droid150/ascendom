# Ascendom — Project Guide for Claude

Pixel-art, kingdom-themed habit tracker. Real habits power a hero that rises
and falls through the ranks of a medieval kingdom.

**Pitch:** "Naik setiap hari, kuasai kerajaanmu."
**Brand idea:** Ascend + Kingdom. Upward motion in every celebratory moment.
**Audience:** Gen Z (16–25). Tone: playful, dramatic-royal, never preachy.
**Default language:** Indonesian (`id`), secondary English (`en`).

## Current status

**Phase 1 complete.** Engine implemented with 258 passing tests and 98%+
statement coverage. Phase 2 (sprites) next.

Phases: 1 Foundation/engine → 2 Sprites → 3 MVP app → 4 Depth (boss, kingdom,
companions, bad habits) → 5 Cloud/social (skipped: optional) → 6 Portfolio polish.

Phases 1–4 are local-first and must work fully offline. No backend.

### Notes on versions

- TypeScript is pinned to `~6.0.3` by the Expo 58 template rather than 7.x,
  because Expo's own type definitions are tested against it.
- ESLint stays on 9.x because `eslint-config-expo` peers on `^9`.
- Expo removed built-in PWA support. There is no `expo-pwa`; the manifest is
  hand-written in `public/` and the service worker is generated by `workbox-cli`.
- `shapeRendering="crispEdges"` works on web but is a no-op on native
  react-native-svg. Native crispness comes from integer coordinates and scale.

## Hard architectural rules

1. **`src/engine/` is pure.** Zero imports from React, React Native, Expo, or
   the store — enforced by a `no-restricted-imports` ESLint rule. date-fns is
   permitted. The engine never calls `new Date()` for "now"; time is injected.
2. **No game rules in UI components.** Components read derived values from
   hooks; hooks call the engine. A component that computes XP is a bug.
3. **No magic numbers** outside `engine/constants.ts` and `theme/` tokens.
4. **No `any`.** Banned by a `no-restricted-syntax` lint rule. Explicit return
   types on exported functions.
5. **All user-facing strings go through i18n.** No literal copy in components.
6. **Components under 200 lines.** Extract logic into hooks.
7. **Errors are visible.** Surface via Toast; never swallow silently.
8. **Persisted store is versioned** (`version` + `migrate`) from the start.

## Data models

`src/types/index.ts` holds `Hero`, `Habit`, `HabitLog`, `DayResult`,
`Settings`, `Boss`, `Trophy`, `KingdomState`, and the union types.

Additions beyond the original spec, and why:

- `Hero.ownedCosmetics` — the spec only had `equippedCosmetics`, so there was
  nowhere to record a shop purchase.
- `Hero.lastPotionDayKey` — enforces "max one potion per day".
- `DayResult.graceDay` and `DayResult.goldLost` — lets the Royal Chronicle show
  why a day was safe and what a Fall cost.
- `HabitLog.hpRestored` — the HP cap can make a completion heal less than the
  nominal 3 HP, and undo has to return exactly what was granted.
- `Settings.reduceMotion` — needed to honour the accessibility requirement.
- `Habit.isBad` — Phase 4 bad habits.

## Game constants (`src/engine/constants.ts`)

```
HP_MAX 100              MISS_DAMAGE 10          MAX_DAILY_DAMAGE 30
HP_REGEN_PER_COMPLETION 3                       BAD_HABIT_DAMAGE 5
FALL_HP_RESTORE 50      FALL_GOLD_LOSS_PCT 0.10
GRACE_DAYS 3 (inclusive of onboarding day)      MAX_REST_DAYS 1
MAX_SHIELDS 3           SHIELD_COST 150
POTION_COST 80          POTION_HEAL 30
CATCHUP_MAX_DAYS 7      DEFAULT_DAY_START_HOUR 0
STREAK_TIER_DAYS 7      STREAK_BONUS_PER_TIER 0.10   STREAK_BONUS_MAX 0.50
REWARDS easy 10/5, medium 20/10, hard 35/15
```

`MAX_DAILY_DAMAGE` is an addition to the original spec. Without it, damage
scales linearly with habit count, so a user with ten habits is punished five
times harder than one with two for identical behaviour while healing only
scales at 3 HP per completion. The cap is three missed habits' worth.

### XP curve

`xpToNext(level) = round(30 + 12 * (level - 1) ** 1.25)`

Verified against the intended pacing at ~110 XP/day (about five medium habits
with a small streak bonus), asserted in `xp.test.ts`:
level 5 in 1.9 days, level 10 in 8.4 days, level 30 in 99 days, level 45 in
248 days. Excess XP carries over and multiple level-ups per action work.

### Rules settled where the spec was ambiguous

- **Grace period** covers the onboarding day plus the next two (three protected
  days total), compared inclusively against `graceUntilDayKey`.
- **Streak on a protected day freezes** — it survives but does not increment,
  since the user didn't do the work. Buying a shield must not buy streak
  progress. Applies to grace, rest days, and shields.
- **Streak multiplier uses the streak the completion produces**, so the seventh
  consecutive day pays the first +10% immediately.
- **One-time quests** never deal damage, never earn a streak bonus, and are
  auto-archived at rollover.
- **Undo** is current-day only, reverses XP/gold/stat/HP and the streak
  increment, but never lowers `bestStreak`.
- **Protection order** on a missed day: grace → rest day → shield. A shield is
  never spent on a day already protected by the calendar, or when nothing was
  missed.
- **Falls during catch-up** can repeat, at most one per evaluated day. A full
  week of neglect at 30 damage/day costs two levels (asserted in `fall.test.ts`).

### Day rollover

`dayKey = format(subHours(date, settings.dayStartHour), 'yyyy-MM-dd')`

On app start and foreground, evaluate `lastEvaluatedDayKey + 1 → yesterday`,
capped at `CATCHUP_MAX_DAYS`; older days are forgiven and `forgivenDays > 0`
triggers the welcome-back screen. Today is never evaluated. Habits are skipped
for a day if archived, bad, not scheduled that weekday, or created afterwards.

`evaluateMissedDays` is pure and deterministic — asserted by a test that calls
it twice with identical input.

## Design system

Palette: Sweetie 16, indexed in `src/theme/colors.ts` (order is load-bearing —
sprites index into it). Base `#1a1c2c`, gold `#ffcd75`, HP red `#b13e53`,
XP green `#38b764`, accent blue `#41a6f6`, parchment panels for content.
Press Start 2P for titles/numbers only; Pixelify Sans for body (min 14px).
4px spacing grid, `border.radius` is 0 everywhere, 2px hard drop shadows,
stepped/snappy motion. Respect `settings.reduceMotion`.
Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast, `MIN_TOUCH_SIZE` 44, screen-reader labels from
the `a11y.*` i18n keys, never colour-only signalling.

## Sprites (Phase 2)

32×32 char grids (`string[]`, `.` = transparent), integer scale only.
Layer order: shadow → body → outfit → hair → headgear → weapon → cape → effects.
Skin tones (5+) and hair colours (6+) are **palette swaps**, not separate art.
`compose.ts` must run-length-merge horizontal runs into wide rects — 1024
one-pixel rects per sprite will not perform, and `/dev/sprites` renders many
sprites at once. Keep a `SpriteSource` abstraction so PNG spritesheets can
replace generated grids without touching screens.

## Voice

A slightly dramatic royal advisor. Short sentences, light humour, Gen Z
friendly, never shaming. Penalties are theatrical. All copy lives in
`src/i18n/locales/{id,en}.json`.
